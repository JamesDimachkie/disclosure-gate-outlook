Guardcell OCR assets

tesseract.js 7.0.0 and tesseract.js-core 7.0.0: Apache-2.0.
https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js-core
The core compiles Tesseract and Leptonica and their bundled image codecs.
The unmodified distribution worker and its bundled dependency notices are included.

English traineddata: @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0, 4.0.0_best_int.
https://github.com/naptha/tessdata/tree/gh-pages
https://github.com/naptha/tessdata/blob/gh-pages/LICENSE (Apache-2.0)
The npm wrapper package declares MIT but contains no licence file. The data repository
licenses the traineddata under Apache-2.0; that licence is included alongside the model.

Only the LSTM engine is used. The portable non-SIMD core avoids downloading a choice of
cores. Workers, core, WASM and English model resolve to this add-in's own static origin.
No email content or image is sent for OCR. No traineddata or email cache is written to
IndexedDB by Tesseract (cacheMethod none). Item sessionData holds bounded OCR results.
